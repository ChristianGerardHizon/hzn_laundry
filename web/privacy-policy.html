Privacy Policy

Hi-Zone Laundry · Last updated: August 28, 2026

Overview

Hi-Zone Laundry (“the App”) is a business application used by authorized staff of Hi-Zone laundry locations to manage orders, customers, inventory, and daily store operations. The App is provided by HznSystems.

This policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and the choices available to you. The App is intended for business use by staff members, not for general public consumer sign-up.

Information we collect

  • Account information — name, email address, and login credentials for authorized staff accounts.
  • Business and customer records — information entered in the course of laundry operations, such as customer names, order details, payments, branch assignments, and related store records.
  • Device and app diagnostics — crash reports, error logs, performance data, and limited session replay data used to fix bugs and improve reliability. Diagnostic data is processed through Sentry.
  • Local device data — preferences such as selected branch, printer settings, and sign-in tokens stored securely on your device.

Device permissions

The Android app may request the following permissions:

  • Internet — to sync data with your organization’s server and check for app updates.
  • Bluetooth and nearby devices — to connect to supported thermal receipt printers used at the store.
  • Location (while using the app) — required by Android for Bluetooth device discovery on some devices.
  • Storage / files — to save or share receipts, exports, and attachments when you use those features.

The App does not use permissions to track your personal location for advertising or unrelated purposes.

How we use information

  • Operate laundry order, POS, inventory, and reporting features.
  • Authenticate staff and enforce role-based access.
  • Print receipts and store documents you choose to generate.
  • Maintain security, troubleshoot issues, and improve app stability.
  • Deploy required app updates and minimum supported versions.

Where data is stored

Business data is stored on servers operated for Hi-Zone Laundry (hosted infrastructure used by HznSystems). Data is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. Local copies may be kept on your device for performance and offline resilience where supported.

Sharing of information

We do not sell personal information. Data may be shared only with:

  • Service providers that help us host, secure, or monitor the App (for example, error reporting).
  • Your organization’s authorized administrators and staff, according to app roles.
  • Legal or regulatory authorities when required by law.

Data retention

Business records are retained according to your organization’s operational needs and applicable record-keeping requirements. Diagnostic logs are retained for a limited period needed to investigate and resolve issues.

Security

We use access controls, encrypted transport, and secure credential storage appropriate for a business management application. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect data against unauthorized access.

Children

The App is not directed to children and is not intended for use by anyone under 13 years of age.

Your choices

  • Staff access is managed by your organization’s administrators.
  • You may revoke Bluetooth or storage permissions in Android settings, though some features may stop working.
  • Contact your branch or system administrator to request correction or deletion of business records where applicable.

Changes to this policy

We may update this page from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top will reflect the latest revision. Continued use of the App after changes means you accept the updated policy.
